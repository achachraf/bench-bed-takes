# bench-bed takes and remixes

[bench-bed](https://bench.achraf.app) gives the same prompt to different AI models and publishes every app they build, unedited.

This repository collects what visitors think of those apps, and their attempts to do better.

## How it works

1. **Give your take.** Open an app on the site and use "Give your take" on its card. Say what it got right or wrong against the brief.
2. **Get approved.** Approved takes appear on the app's card with your GitHub handle. An approved take earns you one remix of that app.
3. **Remix.** Send one prompt edit: add one paragraph, or replace one, with at most 400 characters of new text.
4. **One run.** The edited prompt runs once, on the same model and setup as the original. No retries and no hand edits.
5. **Join the bench.** If the result is approved, it appears next to the original with your edit highlighted and your name on it.

Originals never change. Remixes are always shown as a second take, never as a replacement.

## Labels

- `take`, `remix`: set by the forms.
- `approved`: the take or remix is accepted and will appear on the site.
- `declined`: not accepted. The issue says why.
